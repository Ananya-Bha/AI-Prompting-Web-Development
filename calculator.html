📧 Gmail Instructions

  1. Open Gmail in your web browser or app
  2. Click on "Sent" in the left sidebar
  3. Look at the date range - count emails sent in the last 7 days
  4. Divide that number by 7 to get your daily average
  5. Alternatively, check your "Sent" folder and estimate based on recent activity

📧 Outlook Instructions

  1. Open Outlook (web, desktop, or mobile app)
  2. Navigate to "Sent Items" folder
  3. Filter by date to see emails sent in the past week
  4. Count the total and divide by 7 for daily average
  5. Or check your sent folder and estimate based on your typical usage

📧 Other Email Clients

  1. Open your email app (Apple Mail, Yahoo Mail, etc.)
  2. Go to your "Sent" or "Sent Items" folder
  3. Review emails sent in the past week
  4. Count and divide by 7, or make a reasonable estimate

📺 Netflix Instructions

  1. Open Netflix in your browser or app
  2. Click on your profile icon (top right)
  3. Go to "Account" → "Viewing Activity"
  4. Review your watch history for the past week
  5. Add up total hours and divide by 7 for daily average

📺 YouTube Instructions

  1. Open YouTube and sign in
  2. Click on your profile icon (top right)
  3. Go to "Time watched" or check "History"
  4. YouTube shows your weekly watch time
  5. Divide weekly hours by 7 for daily average

🎵 Spotify / Apple Music Instructions

  1. Open your music streaming app
  2. Check "Recently Played" or listening history
  3. Estimate hours spent listening per day
  4. Or use your device's Screen Time (iOS) or Digital Wellbeing (Android)

📱 Using Device Screen Time

  1. iOS: Settings → Screen Time → See All Activity
  2. Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Dashboard
  3. Look for streaming apps (Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, etc.)
  4. Add up daily hours across all streaming apps

💻 PC Gaming (Steam, Epic Games, etc.)

  1. Open Steam (or your gaming platform)
  2. Go to your profile → "Games" tab
  3. Check "Playtime" for each game
  4. Review your recent gaming sessions
  5. Estimate daily hours based on weekly playtime

🎮 Xbox Instructions

  1. Open Xbox app or go to xbox.com
  2. Sign in and go to your profile
  3. Check "Achievements" or "Game Activity"
  4. Review your gaming history for the past week
  5. Calculate daily average from weekly hours

🎮 PlayStation Instructions

  1. Open PlayStation app or sign in to playstation.com
  2. Go to your profile → "Games"
  3. Check "Trophies" or gaming activity
  4. Review recent gaming sessions
  5. Estimate daily hours from weekly activity

📱 Mobile Gaming Instructions

  1. iOS: Settings → Screen Time → See All Activity
  2. Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Dashboard
  3. Look for gaming apps in your screen time
  4. Add up hours across all gaming apps

☁️ Google Drive Instructions

  1. Go to drive.google.com or open Google Drive app
  2. Click on "Storage" in the left sidebar (or bottom on mobile)
  3. You'll see "X GB of 15 GB used" (or your plan limit)
  4. The number shown is your total storage used in GB
  5. This includes Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos

☁️ OneDrive (Microsoft) Instructions

  1. Go to onedrive.live.com or open OneDrive app
  2. Click on your profile icon (top right)
  3. Go to "Settings" → "Storage"
  4. You'll see "X GB of Y GB used"
  5. Note the GB used number

☁️ iCloud Instructions

  1. On iPhone/iPad: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
  2. On Mac: System Preferences → Apple ID → iCloud
  3. On web: icloud.com → Account Settings
  4. Look for "Storage" section showing "X GB of Y GB"
  5. The first number is your total storage used

☁️ Dropbox Instructions

  1. Go to dropbox.com or open Dropbox app
  2. Click on your profile icon (top right)
  3. Go to "Settings" → "Plan"
  4. You'll see storage usage: "X GB of Y GB used"
  5. Note the GB used

☁️ Other Cloud Services

  1. Open your cloud storage app or website
  2. Look for "Storage", "Account", or "Settings"
  3. Find the storage usage information
  4. Note the total GB used (not the limit)

🌐 Browser Downloads Instructions

  1. Chrome: Press Ctrl+Shift+J (Windows) or Cmd+Option+J (Mac) → Network tab
  2. Firefox: Press F12 → Network tab → Check data transferred
  3. Check your browser's download history
  4. Estimate total GB downloaded in the past week, divide by 7

☁️ Cloud Drive Uploads Instructions

  1. Check your Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox activity
  2. Look for recent file uploads
  3. Add up file sizes (photos, videos, documents)
  4. Estimate daily uploads: weekly total ÷ 7

📱 Mobile Data Usage Instructions

  1. iOS: Settings → Cellular → Scroll to see app data usage
  2. Android: Settings → Network & Internet → Data Usage
  3. Check upload/download data for apps
  4. Divide monthly data by 30 for daily average

💻 Network Settings (Windows) Instructions

  1. Open Settings → Network & Internet
  2. Click "Data usage"
  3. View data usage by app
  4. Check upload/download statistics

💻 Network Settings (Mac) Instructions

  1. Open Activity Monitor (Applications → Utilities)
  2. Go to "Network" tab
  3. Check "Data sent" and "Data received"
  4. Estimate daily usage from weekly totals
