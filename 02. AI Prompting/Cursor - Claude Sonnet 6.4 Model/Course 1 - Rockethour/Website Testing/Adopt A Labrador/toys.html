What toys do Labradors Love to play with?

Tennis Ball

A classic favorite! Labradors love chasing and retrieving tennis balls.

Kong Toy

Durable and great for stuffing with treats. Keeps Labradors entertained for hours.

Frisbee

Perfect for outdoor play and exercise. Labradors excel at catching frisbees mid-air!
