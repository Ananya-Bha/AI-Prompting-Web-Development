About Us

Transforming environmental awareness into measurable action through innovative technology solutions.

In a world where the average person's carbon footprint is approximately 7 tonnes CO₂-equivalent per year, and global freshwater use has increased nearly six-fold since 1900, the call for accessible tools to empower everyday sustainability has never been louder. Meanwhile, around 30% of water is wasted annually — about 9.5 trillion litres — and more than 40% of the global population is projected to face severe water shortages by 2050. The challenge isn't just awareness, it's translating intent into measurable action — and that's precisely where our app steps in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EcoQuest and sustainable living

What is EcoQuest and why should I use it?

EcoQuest is an interactive platform that helps people measure their ecological footprint and live more sustainably. It tracks carbon, water, and waste usage, sets eco-friendly goals, and provides personalized tips with fun gamification features like streaks and badges.

Who is EcoQuest designed for?

Our main audience is students and young people (ages 12–25) who want to learn about sustainability in a fun and engaging way. However, anyone interested in reducing their ecological impact can use EcoQuest.

How does the ecological footprint quiz work?

The quiz has 16 multiple-choice questions across 8 lifestyle areas such as diet, transport, water, waste, and more. Each answer is scored between 1 (eco-friendly) and 5 (least eco-friendly). At the end, you get a footprint percentage, a category (Low, Moderate, or High), and personalized advice on how to improve.

What kind of tips and challenges will I get?

You'll receive eco-tips tailored to your lifestyle. Examples include "Take shorter showers," "Try a meat-free day," or "Carry a reusable bottle." You can also set challenges like "Use 50% less plastic this month," and track streaks to stay motivated.

Can I track my progress over time?

Yes! The dashboard shows charts and stats of your impact over time. You'll see changes in your carbon, water, and waste usage, along with progress towards your goals and comparisons to your past results.

How do reminders work?

When you save or "try" an eco-tip, EcoQuest can create a reminder that syncs with your Google Calendar. These reminders also appear on your dashboard so you never miss a chance to stay on track.

How is EcoQuest different from other footprint calculators?

Unlike most calculators that only give you a score once, EcoQuest combines the quiz with ongoing tracking, goal setting, gamification, and Google Calendar integration. This makes it practical, engaging, and habit-forming rather than a one-time activity.
