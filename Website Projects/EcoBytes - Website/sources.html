How the Calculator Works

Each digital activity has a different carbon cost depending on energy use, data transfer, and data centre emissions. Our calculator uses research-based emission factors to estimate your digital carbon footprint.

1

Data Collection

The calculator collects your daily digital activity data: number of emails sent, hours of streaming and gaming, cloud storage used, and data transferred.

2

Emission Calculation

Each activity is multiplied by its corresponding CO₂ emission factor (grams of CO₂ per unit of activity).

3

Total Footprint

All individual emissions are added together to calculate your total daily digital carbon footprint in grams of CO₂.

4

Time Conversion

The daily total is converted into weekly and yearly values to help you understand the long-term impact of your digital habits.

Limitations & Accuracy Disclaimer

This calculator provides an estimated digital footprint intended for awareness and learning, not precise carbon accounting.

⚠️
Results are estimates, not exact values. Actual emissions can vary significantly based on multiple factors.
🌐
Internet infrastructure differs by country. The energy mix (renewable vs. non-renewable) in your region affects actual emissions.
🔌
Device efficiency varies. Older devices and less efficient hardware consume more energy than newer, optimized devices.
📡
Network conditions matter. Data transfer efficiency depends on connection quality, distance to servers, and network congestion.
⚙️
Service providers differ. Different streaming platforms, email providers, and cloud services have varying energy efficiency.
