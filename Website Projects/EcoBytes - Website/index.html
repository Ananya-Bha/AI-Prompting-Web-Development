Every click, stream and message uses electricity in data centres, networks and devices, and most of that electricity still comes from fossil fuels. This creates a digital carbon footprint – the greenhouse gas emissions from producing, powering and connecting our phones, laptops and online services.

Global internet use consumes more than 400 terawatt-hours of electricity every year, which is more than the entire annual electricity use of some countries.

Digital Carbon Footprint Calculator. Innovation.

EcoBytes is a student-built web project designed to make the invisible impact of our online lives visible. Using published averages from organisations like the Carbon Trust, ADEME, the International Energy Agency and others, we estimate CO₂ emissions from everyday digital habits.

The goal is not to scare people, but to give them clear information and simple actions they can take – like reducing unnecessary emails, lowering video quality when it's not needed, or cleaning up old files.
