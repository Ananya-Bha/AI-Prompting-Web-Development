Blue Collar Jobs
What Are Blue Collar Jobs?
Blue collar jobs are manual labor jobs, often requiring physical work. They are usually found in industries like construction, manufacturing, plumbing, and electrical work.
Key Characteristics
  • Hands-on tasks with tools/machines
  • Often require vocational training or apprenticeship
  • Paid hourly or by job
  • Physical work in varied environments (indoors/outdoors)
Pros & Cons
Pros Cons
Pros Cons
  • Skills-based, less focus on degrees
  • Quick entry to workforce
  • Growing demand in trades
  • Overtime and union benefits available
  • Physically demanding
  • Risk of injury
  • Lower starting pay
  • Less flexibility in schedule
