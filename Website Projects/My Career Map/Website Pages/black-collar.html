Black Collar Jobs
What Are Black Collar Jobs?
Black collar jobs typically refer to manual labor in hazardous or industrial environments, especially mining, oil & gas, or extraction industries.
Key Characteristics
  • Tough physical work, often in dangerous environments
  • Can involve shifts, heavy machinery, underground or offshore sites
  • Require technical training and certifications
Pros & Cons
Pros Cons
  • High wages due to risk level
  • Hands-on skill development
  • Often unionized (better benefits)
  • High risk of physical injury or exposure to hazardous materials
  • Tough working conditions
  • Remote work locations
