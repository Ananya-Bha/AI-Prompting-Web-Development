Green Collar Jobs
What Are Green Collar Jobs?
Green collar jobs are focused on environmental sustainability. These roles help improve the planet through clean energy, conservation, and eco-friendly technologies.
Key Characteristics
  • Involve science, policy, or hands-on eco work
  • High growth industry (solar, wind, conservation)
  • Jobs in private, public, and nonprofit sectors
Pros & Cons
Pros Cons
  • Help combat climate change
  • Increasing global demand
  • Good long-term growth
  • May require specialized training or STEM degrees
  • Jobs can be physically demanding (fieldwork)
