About Us

Welcome to My Career Map — your personal compass to career discovery.

We believe that every person deserves a fulfilling career that aligns with who they are — not just what they scored in school. Whether you're a student unsure of what comes next, or just someone exploring possibilities, My Career Map is here to guide you.

Through a smart, interactive quiz and tailored insights, we help you discover which career "collar" best suits your personality, values, and preferences. With engaging questions and data-driven results, we offer clarity in a confusing world of work.

Our mission?
To simplify career decisions and empower future professionals — one quiz at a time.

Features

🧠

Smart Career Quiz

Answer 20 thoughtfully designed questions that analyze your work style, interests, and strengths — leading you to one of eight career categories ("collars").

🎯

Personalized Results

At the end of the quiz, you’ll discover which collar career path suits you best — complete with detailed explanations, strengths, and job roles.

🔍

Explore Career Paths

Our website is designed to be simple, fast, and fun — no clutter, no confusion. Perfect for students, career counselors, or anyone seeking career clarity.

🌐

Built for Exploration

Not sure where you fit? You can retake the quiz, explore other collar paths, or deep dive into different career pages anytime.

🆓

Free and Accessible

No sign-up. No paywall. Just honest, helpful guidance to point you in the right direction.
