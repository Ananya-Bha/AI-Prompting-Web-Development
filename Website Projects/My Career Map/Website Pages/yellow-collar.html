Yellow Collar Jobs
What Are Yellow Collar Jobs?
Yellow collar jobs are generally associated with creative and artistic professions, including designers, artists, writers, and media professionals.
Key Characteristics
  • Heavily relies on imagination, design, or media production
  • Project-based, freelance, or entrepreneurial formats are common
  • Dynamic work environments, often online or hybrid
Pros & Cons
Pros Cons
  • High creative freedom and personal expression
  • Flexible work arrangements (freelance, remote)
  • Impactful and fulfilling for artistic minds
  • Unstable income (especially for freelancers)
  • Competitive industry
  • Requires strong self-marketing and networking
