THE SUN

Our Celestial Guardian

"In the vast tapestry of the cosmos, no celestial body commands greater reverence than our Sun—the brilliant star at the heart of our solar system."

Ancient Light

The sunlight that warms your face today began its journey 8 minutes and 20 seconds ago. Yet the energy within that light was born in the Sun's core over 100,000 years ago, slowly working its way outward through countless collisions before finally reaching us.

Magnificent Scale

The Sun contains 99.86% of all the mass in our solar system. So vast is our star that approximately 1.3 million Earths could fit within its sphere. At its core, temperatures reach a staggering 15 million degrees Celsius, where nuclear fusion transforms hydrogen into helium.

Eternal Cycles

Every 11 years, the Sun's magnetic field completely flips—north becomes south and south becomes north. During this cycle, solar activity waxes and wanes, creating spectacular auroras on Earth when charged particles collide with our atmosphere. This celestial dance has continued for 4.6 billion years.
