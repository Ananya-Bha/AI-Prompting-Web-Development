🐭 Pygmy Shrews

Fun Facts About Pygmy Shrews!

🏆 The Etruscan pygmy shrew is the world's smallest mammal by mass!

💨 They have an incredibly fast metabolism and must eat every 2 hours or they'll die!

💓 Their hearts beat up to 1,200 times per minute! That's 20 beats per second!

🍽️ They eat up to 3 times their own body weight in food every day!

⚡ Despite being tiny, they're fierce hunters and can take down prey larger than themselves!
