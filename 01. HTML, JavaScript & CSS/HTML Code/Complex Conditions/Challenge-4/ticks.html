🕷️ Ticks

Fun Facts About Ticks!

🐛 Ticks are actually arachnids, not insects! They're related to spiders and scorpions!

⏰ A tick can survive for up to a year without eating!

🔬 There are over 900 species of ticks around the world!

🌡️ Ticks can detect body heat, moisture, and carbon dioxide from far away!
