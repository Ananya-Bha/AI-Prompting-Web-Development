🐀 Rats

Fun Facts About Rats!

🧠 Rats are incredibly intelligent! They can learn tricks, solve puzzles, and even play hide-and-seek!

😊 Rats laugh when they're tickled! Scientists have recorded their ultrasonic giggles!

🤝 Rats are social animals and can become depressed if left alone.

🦷 A rat's teeth never stop growing! They must gnaw on things to keep them short.
