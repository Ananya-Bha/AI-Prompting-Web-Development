🧁 Muffins

Wait... Muffins Aren't Animals!

😄 Did you seriously think muffins were animals? That's hilarious!

🎂 But since you're here, let's learn about muffins anyway!

📜 The word "muffin" comes from the French word "moufflet" meaning soft bread!

🫐 Blueberry muffins are the most popular muffin flavor in the world!

🍽️ The difference between a muffin and a cupcake? Cupcakes are frosted, muffins are not!
