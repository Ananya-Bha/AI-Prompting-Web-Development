🐆 Ocelots

Fun Facts About Ocelots!

🌙 Ocelots are nocturnal wild cats that live in South and Central America!

🎨 Every ocelot has a unique pattern of spots, like a fingerprint!

🏊 Unlike most cats, ocelots are excellent swimmers!

🌳 They spend most of their day sleeping in trees and hunt at night!

😺 Ocelots were once kept as pets (even Salvador Dalí had one!), but they're now protected!
