🦟 Fleas

Fun Facts About Fleas!

🏃 Fleas can jump up to 150 times their own height! That's like a human jumping over a 40-story building!

⚡ They can accelerate 50 times faster than a space shuttle!

🕒 A flea can live for more than 100 days without eating!

🥚 Female fleas can lay 2,000 eggs in their lifetime!
